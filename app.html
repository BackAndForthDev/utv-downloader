AirPlay or a TV without Cast?

Chrome can't AirPlay on any system, so the extension can't either. The quickest full-quality route:

  • Mac or iPhone: download as MP4, open it (History → ▶ Play opens QuickTime on a Mac) and press the AirPlay button.
  • Windows: download as MP4, open it in Films & TV (History → ▶ Play) and use Cast to device, or right-click the file → Cast to Device. Most smart TVs, including Hisense, accept this.
  • Linux: download as MP4 and play it from VLC's Playback → Renderer menu (Chromecast), or use your TV's DLNA/media-server app.
